02 — The Craft

Wood, patience,
and a good eye.

Dream Furniture started as a small workshop with a few hand tools and a simple belief: furniture should be made slowly, from real materials, by people who will still be here when you need it repaired.

Today we build everything on site in Addis Ababa — cutting, joining, sanding and finishing under one roof, so nothing leaves the workshop until it's right.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We start with your room, your measurements and how you actually live in it.

  2. 02

    Selection

    We choose the timber and finish together — grain, tone and durability.

  3. 03

    Joinery

    Frames are cut and joined by hand, then checked square before assembly.

  4. 04

    Finishing

    Hand-sanded and oiled in several passes for a finish that ages with use.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Delivered and assembled in your home, across Addis Ababa.
